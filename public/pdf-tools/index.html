PDF Toolkit

Four browser-only PDF tools for institutional paperwork. Nothing is uploaded to a server — every document is opened, edited and saved inside your own browser.

🗂️

PDF Studio

The all-in-one workspace: organise, split, watermark, number and compress.

  • Rearrange, rotate, delete pages
  • Extract a page range
  • Compress scanned PDFs
🔗

Merge PDFs

Stitch several PDFs into one, in the order you choose, with a letterhead logo.

  • Drag to reorder files
  • Logo on first or every page
  • Page numbering
🖼️

PDF to Images

Export pages as JPG, PNG or WebP at screen or print resolution.

  • Pick the pages you need
  • 72 to 600 DPI
  • One ZIP or separate files
✍️

Sign & Stamp

Draw, type or upload a signature and place it exactly where it belongs.

  • Drag to position on the page
  • Designation and date line
  • Repeat on every page
Client-side only · built for JSS Polytechnic, Mysuru · signatures added here are visual, not cryptographic digital signatures.
